Privacy Policy

Forgotten Songs: Dice  ·  Effective date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Forgotten Songs: Dice (the "App"), a dice roller for the Forgotten Songs board game developed by Core Helper Lab ("Developer", "we", "us"), handles user information. In short: the App works fully offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Section 1

No Data Collection

The App has no user accounts, no backend servers, and no network connection. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any information about you or your device.

The App does not include:
  • registration or sign-in;
  • analytics, crash reporting, or tracking;
  • advertising or advertising identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA);
  • in-app purchases or subscriptions;
  • third-party SDKs that transmit data.
Section 2

Data Stored on Your Device

To work between launches, the App keeps a small amount of data locally on your device:

  • the history of your dice rolls;
  • the success threshold you have set;
  • app preferences (sound, vibration, language).

This data never leaves your device through the App and is not accessible to us. It may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google backup) according to your system settings; such backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies.

Section 3

Device Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, contacts, location, or the internet. Sound and haptic feedback use standard system functions and require no permissions. While the App is open, it keeps the screen from dimming so the dice stay visible at the table; this also requires no permission.

Section 4

External Links

The App's information screen shows a QR code leading to the publisher's website (kologames.start.page) with links to the game's social media pages. Tapping the QR code opens this website in your device's browser.

The App does not load the page itself and sends no data about you along with the link. Once the page is open in your browser, the website and the social media services it links to are governed by their own privacy policies.

Section 5

Data Deletion

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request from us. You can remove the data stored on your device at any time:

  • clear the roll history in the App;
  • delete the App — this removes all of its local data.
Section 6

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Section 7

Changes to Policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, we will update this Policy and its effective date before that version is released.
